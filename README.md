The DeepRunAway repository is home to a suite of approaches that enrich relativistic "runaway" electron descriptsions with physics-constrained deep learning methods to provide efficient runaway electron surrogate models. 

## DeepRunAway Models
### Adjoint-driven runaway electron surrogates
- An adjoint formulation of the kinetic relativistic Fokker-Planck equation is exploited to provide an efficient means of directly computing quantities of interest pertaining to a runaway electron distribution
### Relativistic Fokker-Planck surrogates
- The relativistic Fokker-Planck equation is directly solved in various configurations to provide runaway electron distribution information

## Physics-Constrained Deep Learning Approaches
- Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) are used with high customization to learn solutions across a range of parameters, offering parametric PDE solutions
