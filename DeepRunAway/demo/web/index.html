DeepRunAway demo

Runaway-probability PINN surrogate

This browser demo evaluates a physics-informed neural-network surrogate for the runaway probability function using ONNX Runtime. The model predicts the scalar runaway probability over momentum/pitch-angle space and evaluates the corresponding PDE residual on the same grid.

What the demo shows

The learned scalar is the runaway probability P(p, ξ; |Eφ|, Zeff, α), where p is the normalized relativistic momentum and ξ is the pitch-angle cosine. The left panel shows the predicted runaway probability, while the right panel shows the magnitude of the strong-form PDE residual.

The controls below vary the electric-field magnitude |Eφ|, effective charge Zeff, and synchrotron-radiation parameter α. The plots use a fixed 100 × 100 log-energy/pitch-angle grid and run locally in the browser with the WASM backend.

Model and training context

  • Low-energy boundary condition: P(p_min, ξ) = 0 is hard-enforced by the output transform.
  • High-energy runaway boundary: P(p_max, ξ) = 1 is applied on the inflow portion of the high-energy boundary where U_p > 0.
  • Reduced O-X threshold gating can be used during training to avoid parameter regimes where the strong-form solution becomes discontinuous.
  • The exported ONNX models are used directly by this page: rpf_forward.onnx for the probability and rpf_residual.onnx for the residual.
